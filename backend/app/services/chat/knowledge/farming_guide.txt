# Ethiopian Farming Guide

## Soil Preparation
- Plow 2-3 times before planting
- Apply organic manure (compost) 2-3 weeks before planting
- Test soil pH and adjust with lime if needed (optimal pH: 6.0-7.0)

## Planting
- Use certified seeds from reputable sources
- Plant at the right time based on local rainfall patterns
- Plant at recommended depth (varies by crop)
- Ensure proper seed spacing for optimal growth

## Water Management
- Water crops during dry spells
- Use efficient irrigation methods (drip, furrow, sprinkler)
- Avoid water logging (can cause root rot)
- Practice rainwater harvesting techniques

## Pest and Disease Management
- Regular field inspection (weekly recommended)
- Early detection and treatment of pests/diseases
- Use integrated pest management (IPM) approach
- Apply pesticides only when necessary and follow safety guidelines

## Harvesting
- Harvest at the right maturity stage
- Use appropriate harvesting equipment
- Handle produce gently to minimize damage
- Harvest early morning or late afternoon

## Post-Harvest Handling
- Clean and sort produce immediately after harvest
- Proper drying to reduce moisture content
- Store in pest-free, well-ventilated areas
- Use hermetic storage bags for grain protection

## Crop Rotation Benefits
- Prevents soil nutrient depletion
- Reduces pest and disease buildup
- Improves soil structure
- Increases overall farm productivity

## Recommended Rotation Cycles
- Year 1: Maize + Beans
- Year 2: Wheat + Peas
- Year 3: Barely + Vetch
- Year 4: Teff + Lupine

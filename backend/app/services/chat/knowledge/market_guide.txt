# Ethiopian Market Guide

## Major Markets in Ethiopia
- Addis Ababa (largest market)
- Bahir Dar (Amhara regional hub)
- Adama (Oromia commercial center)
- Mekelle (Tigray market)
- Hawassa (Sidama hub)
- Jimma (coffee region)

## Selling Tips
- Monitor market prices regularly
- Sell when prices are favorable (post-harvest prices lower)
- Consider forming cooperatives for better prices
- Use digital platforms for market access

## Price Trends
- Prices typically lower immediately after harvest
- Prices rise during pre-harvest (shortage period)
- Quality affects price significantly
- Regional price variations exist

## Export Opportunities
- Coffee (major export crop)
- Sesame
- Horticulture products (flowers, vegetables)
- Spices
- Oilseeds
